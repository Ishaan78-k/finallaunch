# Roadmap
Phase 2 party management -> 3 elections -> 4 Prime Minister mode (done: budget, economy, cabinet, parliament, coalition stability) -> 5 living world (done: crises, opposition play, repeat elections) -> 6 public protests, police and promises (done: simulation 6A, 3D crowd 6B) -> later: junior partner, manifesto, career summary.

## Phase 5 (done)
- **Crises**: about one every six months while you govern. Nine kinds, three answers each (costly, cheap, nothing). Costs come from the government treasury. A strong minister in the right ministry softens the blow. Unanswered crises lapse after 30 days and hurt. Months wait while a crisis is open.
- **Opposition play**: when a rival governs and you sit in opposition, the Opposition Office lets you hold press conferences, marches, scandal exposes and grassroots drives (party funds, cooldowns). The ruling party's approval drifts month by month. Below 40% you can move a no-confidence motion; a hated government loses MPs to rebellion and may fall.
- **Repeat elections**: a term is five years (1800 days). The campaign opens 90 days before election day, tickets reset, members age, and the rivals drift. Unpopular incumbents lose ground. A fallen government (or a successful motion) lets you call an early election (45 days out).
- **Party donations**: after the first election, funds come in monthly, more when popular and in power.
- Saves are version 5.
